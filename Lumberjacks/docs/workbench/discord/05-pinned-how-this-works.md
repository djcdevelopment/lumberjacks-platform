**How this works (read me first)**

One read, and every thread below makes sense.

**Reply rhythm**

I'm one person, currently splitting time across a few projects. I read every thread. Replies
come when I check in — not the minute you post. If a thread goes quiet for a while, that's
pacing, not me ignoring you.

**Tags**

When you post, pick one tag: **question**, **bug**, **claiming a task**, or **first task done**.
That's it — the tag is how I sort my reading pass, so a good tag literally gets you a faster
answer. You'll also see status tags (**needs-derek**, **answered**, **resolved**) come and go on
posts; those are mine (and contributors', for their own tools). Don't worry about them.

**The ownership ladder**

Nothing here requires asking permission to start.

- **0 — Curious.** You read a tool's thread or the catalog page and decide if it's worth your
  evening. Nothing recorded. Reading owes nobody anything.
- **1 — Ran it.** You ran the tool and posted what happened, in its thread — including the part
  where it broke. A report that something failed is worth exactly as much as a report that it
  worked.
- **2 — Fixed one thing.** You completed one of the first tasks listed in a tool's thread and it
  landed. You get credited in `OWNERS.md`; the tool's state moves from unclaimed to **trying**.
- **3 — Contributor.** Two landed changes, or you revived a pruned piece back into something that
  runs, on your own. You get code access to that one piece — not the whole repo — and you triage
  its thread: you're the first read on new replies there. State moves to **claimed**.
- **4 — Owner.** Sustained contribution to a piece over time, and I agree you're the one holding
  it. You set direction for that piece. You can say no to a change — including from me. State
  moves to **owned**.

Most people stay at 0 or 1 forever, and that's the ladder working as intended — this only needs a
few people going further, not everyone.

**What "claimed" means**

Stage 2 and up gets written down in `OWNERS.md` — date, person, which tool, what stage, and a link
back to the evidence (the thread post where it happened). The catalog page shows the same state
next to each tool. If it isn't in the ledger, it isn't claimed yet, no matter what got said in a
thread.

**Stepping back**

If you claimed something and don't want it anymore: one message in the thread saying so. No
explanation required, no guilt. I'll move it back to unclaimed. People's time and energy for this
changes — that's normal, not a letdown.

**Support boundaries**

No DMs for support — everything goes in the tool's thread, even the small stuff. Not because I
don't want to hear from you, but because an answer in a thread helps the next person who hits the
same wall; an answer in a DM helps only you, once.
